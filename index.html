Business problem and approach

A practical, interview-discussable workflow rather than a decorative dashboard.

The problem

What this addresses

Support teams need a consistent way to prioritise enquiries, monitor first response and resolution time, escalate complex issues and communicate clearly with customers.

The build

What is included

This workbook provides a ticket tracker, response templates, illustrative priority-based targets and a dashboard of activity and service metrics.

Data integrity

How to interpret it

Sample records are synthetic. Formulas support the workflow, and the workbook includes a usage guide and documented limitations.
