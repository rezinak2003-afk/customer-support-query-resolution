# SLA & Escalation Matrix

These are **illustrative practice targets only**, not a real company's commitment or policy. Always replace them with an employer-approved service-level agreement before using them with customers.

| Priority | First response target | Resolution target | Example escalation path |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Urgent | 15 minutes | 4 hours | Immediate Tier 2 / team lead for major impact, payment block or safety issue |
| High | 30 minutes | 8 hours | Tier 2 after initial triage for high impact or repeated failure |
| Normal | 120 minutes | 24 hours | Specialist queue when another team is needed or risk of missing target |
| Low | 240 minutes | 48 hours | Team lead when the expected window is exceeded or a supervisor is requested |

## Escalation checklist
1. Confirm ticket ID, category, priority and customer contact channel.
2. Record what was checked and what remains unresolved.
3. Route to the correct queue with relevant information only.
4. Tell the customer the next update time only when it can be honoured.
5. Reassess priority if the customer's impact changes.
6. Record the decision and outcome for auditability.

## Privacy note
Use only the minimum information needed. Never request an OTP, password, PIN or full payment-card number. The project uses fictional tickets and contains no real customer data.
