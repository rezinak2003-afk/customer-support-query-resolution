# Project Case Study — Customer Support Helpdesk

## Business problem
Support teams need a consistent method to triage enquiries, track first-response and resolution timing, route complex cases and maintain clear customer communication across channels.

## Solution built
- A tracker with 30 synthetic tickets across email, chat and web form.
- Ticket category, priority, language, first-response time, elapsed/resolution hours, status, owner and escalation tier.
- Formula-driven first-response and resolution SLA flags linked to a sample SLA matrix.
- A dashboard for ticket volume, active cases, urgent tickets, resolved/closed cases, average elapsed/resolution time, recorded CSAT and first-response SLA attainment.
- 30 bilingual (English/Hindi) reply templates for delivery delays, refunds, damaged items, payment issues, account access, cancellations, wrong items and escalations.
- A sample escalation matrix with priority-based response and resolution targets.

## Core metrics
- Open/active tickets = New + In Progress + Waiting on Customer.
- Resolved/closed = Resolved + Closed.
- Average CSAT = average of scores recorded on resolved/closed examples only.
- First-response SLA attainment = tickets flagged Within SLA divided by total tickets in the sample.

## Tools and skills demonstrated
Excel formulas, lookups, dropdown validation, conditional formatting, dashboard charts, bilingual customer writing, ticket triage, escalation logic and service metrics.

## Data and limitations
All ticket data is synthetic. SLA values are illustrative, not contractual service commitments. The project simulates helpdesk operations in Excel; it is not connected to a Freshdesk/Zendesk account and does not claim real customer-service results.
